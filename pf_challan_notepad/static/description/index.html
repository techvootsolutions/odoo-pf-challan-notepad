Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about installation, compatibility, and features

Does this require Indian HR Payroll?
Yes. PF Challan Notepad requires Odoo's Indian HR Payroll (`l10n_in_hr_payroll`) module to extract salary slip rules and employee UAN information.
Which Odoo versions are supported?
Built for Odoo 18 on Enterprise Edition and Odoo.sh deployments.
How does PF data calculation work?
The module pulls confirmed payslip line items associated with Employee PF, Employer PF, and Employer Pension salary rules to generate the PF Notepad document.
Where can I find the reporting wizard?
Navigate to Indian HR Payroll > Reporting > PF Challan Notepad to launch the date selection wizard.
Does it support multi-company setup?
Yes. Reports are isolated by active company and pull employee contributions assigned to the current active company.
Is INR currency required?
Yes. Indian HR Payroll and EPFO compliance calculations are performed in Indian Rupee (INR) currency.
